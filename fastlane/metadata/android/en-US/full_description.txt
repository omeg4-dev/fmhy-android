An unofficial app for FMHY (FreeMediaHeckYeah), the big community-run list of free stuff on the internet: streaming, downloading, adblocking, reading, software and a lot more.

fmhy.net works on a phone, but it's a big website. This app puts the same list into a native app that's quick to scroll and search and keeps working without a connection. It downloads the wiki's markdown straight from FMHY's GitHub repo and reads it on the phone. There's no server of ours in between, no account, no ads and no tracking.

What you get:
• Every section in the same order as fmhy.net, with the same stars and icons
• One row per line of the wiki. Tap it to see every site on that line with its mirrors and extra links, hold it to open the site
• Search across all ~21,000 sites, with filters for section, starred sites and more
• Bookmarks that stay put when the wiki changes
• Links that FMHY hides behind base64 are decoded for you
• A warning before you open a site that's on FMHY's unsafe list
• Checks for wiki changes when you open it and only downloads what changed
• Light and dark theme, pure black, Material You or your own accent colour, adjustable text size

After the first sync everything is stored on the phone, so browsing and search work offline.

This is a fan project, not made by the FMHY team. Which sites get listed is up to them: suggestions for the wiki go to fmhy.net, problems with the app go to the GitHub issue tracker.
