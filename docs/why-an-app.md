# Why an app, and not just fmhy.net in a browser?

Fair question. The wiki works fine in a browser. The app is for the things a browser can't do because it doesn't know FMHY:

- **Follow a category, get told when it changes.** Pick e.g. Video › Streaming and you get one notification when sites are added or starred there. Change history comes from fmhy/edit and fmhy-tracker.
- **Your saved sites are watched.** If one gets starred, removed or moves to a new link, you hear about it, and removed ones show replacements from the same category.
- **What's new / Removed.** Every addition, star and removal from the last month, by day and section.
- **A browser that blocks ads with uBlock Origin's own filter lists**, plus FMHY's unsafe-sites list, pop-up and redirect blocking, and a bar that tells you where a site is listed on FMHY.
- **Dead site? One tap to a mirror or an alternative** from the same FMHY category.
- **Check any link** against FMHY SafeGuard's lists, from any app, via the share menu.
- **My FMHY:** sort saved sites into your own folders and share them as a list.

Free and open source (GPL-3), no account, no ads, no tracking. Source: https://github.com/omeg4-dev/fmhy-android
